Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At Breavians Technology Pvt. Ltd. ("Breavians", "we", "our", or "us"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website https://www.breavians.com or contact us for IT services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information directly from you when you fill out a form or contact us:

  • Contact details: Name, email address, phone number, and company name.
  • Project details: Information you provide regarding your requirements, budget, or specifications.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited (via analytics).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Responding to your project inquiries and requests for quotation.
  • Delivering, maintaining, and improving our software development and IT services.
  • Communicating project updates, contracts, and invoices.
  • Complying with legal obligations and protecting against fraudulent activities.

3. Data Protection & Confidentiality

We implement industry-standard security practices to safeguard your information. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We are committed to signing Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) to keep proprietary project data confidential.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party providers (such as hosting, email delivery, and website analytics) solely to facilitate our services. These third parties are obligated to keep your data confidential.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by us. To exercise these rights, please contact us at business@breavians.com.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please email us at business@breavians.com.
