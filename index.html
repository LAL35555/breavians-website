FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

How much does a website or app cost?

Cost depends on features, design complexity and platforms. After a free consultation we share a detailed, transparent quote with no hidden charges.

How long does it take to build a project?

A business website typically takes 2–4 weeks, and a mobile app or custom software 6–16 weeks depending on scope. We share a clear timeline before we start.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. We are happy to sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement before you share any details about your idea or business.

Who owns the source code?

You do. Once the project is completed and paid for, full ownership of the source code and intellectual property is transferred to you.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We offer maintenance and support plans covering bug fixes, updates, security patches, monitoring and new feature development.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work with clients across time zones and communicate over email, video calls and project management tools.
